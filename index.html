Meet the Founder

Zena Miller

After spending more than 21 years working in healthcare, I've seen firsthand how important a clean, safe, and well-maintained environment truly is. In medical offices, cleanliness isn't just about appearance. It's about health, trust, attention to detail, and protecting the people who walk through the doors every day.

Throughout my career, I worked in professional healthcare settings where strict standards, organization, and sanitation were non-negotiable. Over the years, I noticed something that stuck with me: the difference that a truly thorough cleaning team can make in the way a workplace functions and feels.

"That experience inspired me to create Zero Trace Pro Cleaning, a locally owned Indiana-based commercial cleaning company built on professionalism, precision, and pride in doing the job the right way."

To provide reliable, detail-driven, and eco-friendly commercial cleaning services that create safe, healthy, and spotless environments for the businesses and communities we serve.
