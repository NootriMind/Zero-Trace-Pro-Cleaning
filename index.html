Meet the Founder
Zena Miller

Founder Zena Miller brings over 21 years of experience in healthcare environments. She believes that "Cleanliness isn't just about appearance — it's about health, trust, and protecting people."

Inspired by seeing the profound difference a truly thorough cleaning team makes in how a workplace functions and feels, she established Zero Trace Pro Cleaning. The company is built on the core values of professionalism, precision, and a deep-rooted Indiana pride.
