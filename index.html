Meet the Founder

Zena Miller

After spending more than 21 years working in healthcare, I've seen firsthand how important a clean, safe, and well-maintained environment truly is. In medical offices, cleanliness isn't just about appearance — it's about health, trust, attention to detail, and protecting the people who walk through the doors every day.

Throughout my career, I worked in professional healthcare settings where strict standards, organization, and sanitation were non-negotiable. Over the years, I noticed something that stuck with me: the difference that a truly thorough cleaning team can make in the way a workplace functions and feels.

That experience inspired me to create Zero Trace Pro Cleaning — a locally owned Indiana-based commercial cleaning company built on professionalism, precision, and pride in doing the job the right way.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, detail-driven, and eco-friendly commercial cleaning services that create safe, healthy, and spotless environments for the businesses and communities we serve.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Zero Trace.

Are you bonded and insured?
Yes. Zero Trace Pro Cleaning is fully bonded and insured. We carry general liability insurance and are bonded for your protection. Documentation is available upon request before your first service.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. We use eco-friendly, EPA-approved products that are safe for your staff, clients, and facility. If you have specific product requirements, let us know and we'll accommodate them.
How does the quoting process work?
Simply fill out our quote request form or give us a call. We'll reach out within 24 hours to discuss your facility's needs. For most commercial spaces, we'll schedule a quick walkthrough to provide an accurate, no-obligation quote tailored to your space.
Are your staff background checked?
Absolutely. Every Zero Trace team member goes through a thorough background check before entering any client facility. We take the trust you place in us seriously — especially in sensitive environments like medical offices and schools.
Can you work around our business hours?
Yes. We offer flexible scheduling including early morning, evening, and weekend cleanings to minimize disruption to your operations. We work around your schedule, not the other way around.
Do you offer one-time or recurring services?
Both. We offer one-time deep cleans, move-in/move-out cleaning, and post-construction cleaning as well as recurring daily, weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly service plans tailored to your needs and budget.
