Logo

The Wordmark

The logo is a custom lockup, not a font-generated wordmark: "ZERO" is always brand blue, "TRACE" switches between dark ink and white depending on background, and the "TM" mark sits tucked against the top of the last letter.

Typography

Type System

Two typefaces, each with one job: Montserrat for anything bold and structural (headings, buttons, labels), Libre Franklin for anything meant to be read at length (paragraphs, descriptions).

Usage

Do & Don't

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo — don't crowd it against edges or other elements
  • Use the dark-background version on photos or navy sections
  • Keep "ZERO" in brand blue, always

Don't

  • Stretch, skew, or resize the wordmark disproportionately
  • Recolor the logo outside the brand palette
  • Place the light-background logo on a busy or low-contrast photo
